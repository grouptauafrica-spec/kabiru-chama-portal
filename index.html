Governance & Code of Laws

Kabiru Chama Constitution & Running Articles

Establishing rules, guidelines, and community program mandates for all members of the Kabiru Chama Account.

Article I: General Membership & Registration

Section 1.1 — Qualification: Any individual dedicated to fighting poverty and fostering long-lasting economic independence is eligible to apply for a Kabiru Chama Account.

Section 1.2 — Entry Fee: New members must submit a one-off registration fee of Ksh 2,000 upon approval.

Section 1.3 — Initiates' Guidance: All members acknowledge the foundational principles set forth by The Three Initiates from the Oloo Inner Circle.

Article II: Savings Structures, Family Tree Wallet & Kabiru Reciprocity

Section 2.1 — Weekly Minimum Deposit: Members commit to saving a minimum of Ksh 1,000 per week per head. Members are permitted and encouraged to save higher amounts.

Section 2.2 — Wallet Custody: All savings deposits, ledger balances, and member withdrawals are exclusively administered through the official Family Tree Wallet.

Section 2.3 — Kabiru Credits Mandate: Members must reciprocate every deposit by acquiring equivalent Kabiru Credits on Kabiru.org to grow their ecosystem balance.

Article III: Joint Ventures, LLPs & Withdrawal Terms

Section 3.1 — Ownership Protection: Savings remain the exclusive personal property of the depositing member and may be withdrawn at any time via the Family Tree Wallet or converted into Joint Venture capital.

Section 3.2 — Venture Maturation: Successful Joint Venture Businesses shall transition into legally registered Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Companies with equity proportional to contribution.

Article IV: Community Programs, Trusts & Sovereign Structure

Section 4.1 — Foundation & Trusts: LLPs mature into Dynasty Trusts and Family Foundations to fund local schools (e.g. TACEP), healthcare, and community infrastructure.

Section 4.2 — Total Sovereignty: Members progressing through the growth spectrum attain full social, spiritual, and economic independence via the official Community Sovereign Program.
